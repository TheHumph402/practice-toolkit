Form 3 Companion (ADASS Structure)

The toolkit mirrors the order of the ADASS Form 3 so that support follows the way assessors actually complete assessments. If you are stuck on a section, click it. The aim is to support thinking, legal defensibility and confidence — not provide templates for copying.
